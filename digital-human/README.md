# Digital Human: Teen Boy (photoreal, Blender 5.3, headless)

A photoreal 3D teenage boy (about 17, 1.75 m) with short wavy dark-brown hair, green eyes, light freckles and a soft closed-mouth smile. He wears a purple crew-neck tee, loose black trousers and white low-top sneakers, and stands relaxed with his hands in his pockets.
Everything is generated by Python scripts (`bpy`) with free tools only. All textures are procedural, so the pipeline needs no image files.

## Outputs (`output/`)
| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `character.blend` | Full scene: character, rig, studio set, lights, 3 cameras, Cycles settings |
| `character.glb` | Posed static export (glTF): meshes, hair as ribbon strips, simple PBR materials and vertex colours |
| `final_render.png` | Hero shot: 85 mm, f/2, 1080x1350, Cycles 192 spp + OIDN, AgX |
| `face_closeup.png` | Face close-up: 85 mm, f/4, focused on the eyes |
| `turntable.mp4` | 360° camera orbit, 96 frames at 24 fps |
| `breakdown.png` | Final render, clay and wireframe side by side |
| `rig_test.png` | The same armature in 2 poses (A-pose rest and the final pose) |

## Pipeline (`scripts/`)
1. `s1_body.py`: **Base body.** Uses the CC0 MakeHuman/MPFB2 base mesh, a clean quad topology with edge loops at the eyes, mouth and joints. MakeHuman macro targets set a male, 17 years old, slim, average-muscle body. The head is scaled to about 7.8 heads tall, and detail targets add a youthful face, soft smile and natural asymmetry. The script builds the 163-bone MPFB "default" rig and its skin weights.
2. `s2_head.py`: **Head and face.**
   - Skin: Principled BSDF with Random-Walk (Skin) subsurface scattering. Vertex masks control redness (nose, cheeks, ears, knuckles), freckles, an oily T-zone (lower roughness plus a clear coat) and the lips. Pores, fine wrinkles and mid-frequency bumps come from bump maps in rest-pose space, so they don't slide when the body is posed. Sheen adds a vellus-hair look.
   - Eyes: a sclera with veins, plus a recessed iris disc with radial fibres, an amber collarette, a limbal ring and a pupil. A separate cornea dome uses glass at IOR 1.376 and lets shadow rays pass through. The eyes also have a caruncle and a wet tear line.
   - Hair: about 60k strand curves (Hair Curves) grown from a crown whorl over a hand-defined hairline. They use guide strands with waves, clumping and a few flyaways, thinning toward the hairline. The material is Principled Hair (Chiang, melanin).
   - Eyebrows: about 1.3k strands. Eyelashes: about 600 strands grown from MakeHuman lash-strip helpers. Peach fuzz: 26k vellus strands on the face.
3. `s3_clothes.py`: **Clothes.** The tee and trousers are separate meshes cut from the body surface and then loosened (the trouser legs become straight tubes). They are skinned to the rig and get thickness from a Solidify modifier. The materials are procedural: cotton jersey with a neck rib, hem stitching and seams for the tee, and black twill with a slanted pocket opening, seams and a fly for the trousers. The sneakers are built on a parametric last fitted to the foot. They have a rubber sole with toe spring and a sidewall groove, laces, eyelets, a leather grain, panel seams with stitching, toe-cap perforations and light scuffs.
4. `s4_pose_sim.py`: **Pose and simulation.** The pose is a contrapposto with weight on the right leg, built with a pelvis tilt and turn plus a counter-tilt in the spine. The legs and arms are solved with IK and baked to FK. The hands go into the front pockets, with the thumbs forward and the fingers relaxed, and the soles are planted on the floor. The cloth simulation runs the trousers first, then freezes them and uses them as a collider for the tee. Growing hand colliders push out pocket bulges, and a "grow" group adds bunching at the hems. Finally, stretched vertices are repaired automatically.
5. `s5_scene.py`: **Studio.** A dark cyclorama with a slightly glossy floor. The lights are a key softbox, a fill, two rim strips, a hair light and a backdrop glow, all colour-temperature driven. There are three 85 mm cameras (hero, face and turntable) with depth of field.
6. `s6_render.py`: renders the final, face, clay, wire, rig-test and turntable images. `s7_export_glb.py` writes the GLB.

```bash
# one-time setup (Python 3.13)
python3.13 -m venv venv && venv/bin/pip install bpy==5.3.0 scipy pillow
pip download anny==0.6.2 --no-deps && unzip anny-0.6.2-py3-none-any.whl -d anny   # MakeHuman/MPFB2 data
# edit env.sh paths, then
./run_all.sh        # body -> head -> clothes -> pose + sim -> scene   (about 2 min on 4 CPU cores)
./render_all.sh     # final renders + turntable                          (about 40-60 min on CPU)
venv/bin/python scripts/s7_export_glb.py
```

## Quality loop (what was checked and fixed)
- **Pass 1:** the hair was orange and frizzy, and the skin was over-exposed. Fix: higher melanin, longer waves, more clumping and lower light energy.
- **Pass 2:** the eyeballs poked through the lids, the lashes looked like a comb and the iris was cartoon-green. Fix: the eye radius now comes from the socket lining, the lashes come from the MakeHuman lash strips, and the iris is a muted hazel-green.
- **Pass 3:** the sneakers showed toes, the cloth sim exploded, the trousers poked through the tee and the neckline had a step. Fix: a parametric shoe last, simulating from the final pose, a sequential trousers-then-tee sim, an armpit pin and harmonic repair, and an elliptical crew neck.
- **Pass 4:** the hair turned blonde under the rim lights, the face close-up was out of focus and the left shoe sank into the floor. Fix: rims reduced by about 70%, focus set on an empty at the eyes, and IK sole planting.

## Honest limits
- The body comes from the MakeHuman base mesh plus morph targets. It isn't a sculpt of this exact person, and the face is close to the reference but not a likeness.
- Skin micro-detail is procedural bump mapping, not a scanned displacement map. At extreme close range the skin still reads slightly CG.
- The clothes are frozen in the final pose after simulation. The armature still deforms the body (see `rig_test.png`), but re-posing needs a new cloth sim (`s4_pose_sim.py`).
- The GLB can't carry Cycles procedural shaders or hair curves. It uses simple PBR, vertex colours and hair ribbon strips. For a game-ready asset you would bake textures.
- The hands are inside the pockets, so the palms and fingers are hidden under the fabric.
